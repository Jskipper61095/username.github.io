Overview

Making people feel
the brand before they touch the product

Luxury retail design is a form of environmental storytelling — every surface, every sightline, every product placement is a decision about how a brand wants to be felt. Across five years within the Estée Lauder Companies portfolio, I worked at every scale of that discipline: from a prestige gifting activation at the Academy Awards to overscale retail installations at Bloomingdales and Bergdorf Goodman, to 3D concept development and launch unit planning for national retail rollouts.

This page documents four distinct bodies of work — each representing a different dimension of luxury brand environment design.

01

Tom Ford Beauty — Oscars Gifting Suite

A private gifting suite curated for Academy Awards weekend — product vignette design, spatial styling, and brand atmosphere at one of the most visible moments in the luxury beauty calendar. The suite balanced editorial restraint with the warmth required of a high-touch hospitality environment.
